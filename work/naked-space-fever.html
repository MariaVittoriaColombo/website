Creative Production

NAKED SPACE × Fever — Primavera Sound, Barcelona

Production and project coordination for a large-scale experiential installation developed by Naked Space for Fever at Primavera Sound Barcelona. Managed logistics, production workflows, supplier coordination, customs documentation, installation planning, and on-site operations. Acted as a link between creative, production and technical teams, ensuring the project was delivered efficiently while adapting to the fast-paced environment of an international music festival.

For Primavera Sound, a circular spatial structure was developed for Fever House, using four large curved elements to create a semi-enclosed, immersive environment. The four inflatable sections form a continuous circular perimeter while leaving two openings that allow people to enter and move through the space. During the night, the installation took on a different visual presence: the blue LED lighting integrated into the structure became particularly visible in the dark, turning the elements into a luminous architectural landmark within the festival.

Inside the structure, the installation became an interactive room dedicated to music and live programming. A sofa created a central area for people to relax, while suspended headphones and screens allowed visitors to listen to and follow performances taking place across the different festival stages.

The space also hosted a dedicated MESA LOBO food stand, offering a menu created specifically for the event.

At the centre of the installation, directly beneath a suspended inflatable ring used as a light, a bar became one of the main gathering points. The space was also used for several DJ sets throughout the event.

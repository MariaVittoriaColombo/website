NAKED SPACE × VASTO Gallery × NIKE

Production support for a site-specific large-scale inflatable installation commissioned by NIKE and presented at Dropcity during Milan Design Week 2026. Coordinated logistics, installation and dismantling processes, supported communication between NIKE, Dropcity and production teams, and contributed to solving on-site challenges to ensure the successful delivery of the project within a highly dynamic exhibition context.

Large-scale installation was developed as a soft, inhabitable landscape within the brand's dedicated area. The structure is a unique piece made of rounded volumes, measuring 13 × 6 m and weighing approximately 230 kg. Its inflated geometry creates a continuous, irregular surface designed to be occupied, allowing visitors to sit and interact directly with the installation.

The installation became the core of the space, transforming the area from a conventional exhibition setting into an environment for gathering and interaction. The structure was positioned centrally, leaving circulation around it while concentrating activity within its soft, responsive volume.

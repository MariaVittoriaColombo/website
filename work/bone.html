Product and Material Research

Bone

Project emerged from ongoing experimentation with movement and the relationship between humans and their environment. At its core lies a modular system: thanks to the dimensional difference between two circumferences, the components can overlap, creating space for a central light source. Interaction is activated by a lighting system embedded in the base, featuring dimmable temperature, an off-timer, and motion detection for automatic activation. The entire piece is made of aluminum, shaped through laser cutting, saddling, and bending techniques.

More than a static object, the project is a reflection of an evolving process, rooted in research, form exploration, and continuous iteration. The final prototype stands as a synthesis of movement, intuition, and experimentation.

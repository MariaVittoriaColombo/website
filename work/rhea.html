Rhea

Rhea is a speculative wearable that stems from a reflection on the concept of care, particularly in relation to women's psychophysical wellbeing. It's not just a piece of technology: it's a symbol of awareness, normalization, and empowerment. Designed to be worn on the body, Rhea merges functionality and aesthetic expression: one part monitors vital signs and cortisol levels, while the other draws inspiration from art and fashion, giving the object a strong personal and visual identity.

The name, Rhea, celebrates femininity and the idea of a device that doesn't just sit on the body, but becomes part of it.

Rather than offering a fixed solution, Rhea aims to spark conversation around health, care, and how we relate to our bodies.
